Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Welcome to Quotezia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Quotezia collects minimal data to provide you with the best experience:

  • Advertising ID: We collect your device's advertising identifier to display personalized advertisements through Google AdMob. This is an anonymous identifier that does not reveal your personal identity.
  • App Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app's performance and user experience.
  • Device Information: Basic device information such as device type and operating system version may be collected for app optimization.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To improve and optimize the app's performance
  • To provide daily quote notifications (if you opt-in)
  • To process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions

3. Third-Party Services

Quotezia uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect and process data according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions.

4. Notifications

Quotezia offers optional daily quote notifications. You can enable or disable notifications at any time through the app settings or your device settings. We do not use notifications for advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

All your preferences, favorite quotes, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not store any personal data on external servers. Your data remains on your device and under your control.

6. Children's Privacy

Quotezia is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Opt-out of personalized advertising through your device settings
  • Reset your advertising identifier at any time
  • Disable notifications through the app or device settings
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the application

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
